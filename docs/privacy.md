**1. Information We Collect**

1.1 **Account Information**: When you register, we collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your username, email address, and password. You may optionally provide additional profile information (e.g., a display name or avatar).

1.2 **User-Submitted Content**: When you post, comment, vote, or otherwise contribute to the Service, we collect and store that content ("User Content"), along with associated metadata such as timestamps, vote counts, and community associations. **Please be aware that User Content you submit to public areas of the Service is visible to all users and the general public.** Do not submit content you wish to keep private.

1.3 **Usage and Log Data**: We automatically collect information about your interactions with the Service, including your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, links clicked, and the date and time of your visit. This data helps us operate, analyze, and improve the Service.

1.4 **Device Information**: We may collect information about the device you use to access the Service, including hardware model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.

1.5 **Communications**: If you contact us (e.g., for support or to report content), we collect the content of your messages and your contact information.

1.6 **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: We use cookies, pixel tags, and similar tracking technologies to recognize you across visits, maintain session state, analyze usage, and personalize your experience. See Section 5 for more detail.

2.1 **Operating the Service**: To create and manage your account, display your User Content, enable community features (posting, commenting, voting), and communicate with you about your account and the Service.

2.5 **Legal Compliance**: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal process, and to exercise or defend legal rights.

2.6 **Machine Learning and AI Model Training**: We may use information collected through the Service — including User Content, usage data, and interactions — to develop, train, fine-tune, evaluate, and improve machine learning and artificial intelligence models and related technologies, whether operated by ExoRealms or its service providers. Where we use personal information for this purpose, we implement appropriate technical safeguards, such as aggregation and de-identification where practicable. User Content submitted to public areas of the Service may be used in its original form as training data, as it has been voluntarily made publicly available.

2.7 **Legitimate Interests**: For any other purpose that is reasonably necessary to operate and grow the Service, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
